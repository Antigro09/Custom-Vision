03Robot & field

Measured transforms

Robot → camera mount, in WPILib axes: +X forward, +Y left, +Z up. Rotation is roll / pitch / yaw in degrees. Zero values are placeholders until measured.

No calibration

Ranging intersects a calibrated camera ray with the measured target plane. Enter the anchor's Z relative to the robot origin (+Z up); leave it blank for 2D results only. Zero is the floor only when the robot origin is on the floor. Match the plane to the selected center or bottom anchor.

Robot +X is forward and +Y is left. Targets need matching camera calibration and a measured mount. Unreliable geometry stays invalid; segmentation outlines and anchors are approximate.

No field layout

WPILib AprilTagFieldLayout JSON: tag IDs, measured field poses, and field dimensions. Check season, origin and units before trusting a field pose.
